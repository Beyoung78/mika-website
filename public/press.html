The bio

Short bio

Mika Montero is a Japanese-American singer, model and creator from Honolulu, now based in Miami. Raised on R&B and soul, she released her debut album No Angel. No Apology. in November 2026, then took a playful detour into house and techno. Her first single Break You Out drops November 23, followed by The Remixes on November 27.

Mika Montero was born in Honolulu in 2000, the daughter of a Japanese mother from Yokohama and an American father from Los Angeles. She grew up with the ocean on her doorstep and a camera in her hand, and by her late teens she was shooting fashion and portraits.

In Los Angeles she studied visual communication and took her first modeling jobs, and a creative side project grew into a career in modeling, photography and content. Today she lives in Miami.

Music was always there, mostly as a private thing. Her home is R&B and soul, the records her father played and the playlists she never stops building. In early November 2026 she released her debut album, No Angel. No Apology. Soon after, a friend dragged her into a house session in Miami. She went in curious and came out laughing, with a new taste for the dance floor.

Break You Out, her first single, out November 23, tells the story of Ruby Rae, an eighty-four-year-old former lounge singer, and one last night at the Starlight Room on Sunset before it is torn down: 90s soul hip-hop meets a 60s torch song. On The Remixes, out November 27, the dance floor takes over, with five versions from Dark Disco Dub to Minimal.

She is warm, observant and a little stubborn, and she likes to keep it that way.
